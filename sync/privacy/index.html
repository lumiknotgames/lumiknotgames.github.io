1. Who we are

Sync ("the app") is a puzzle game published on Google Play by Lumiknot Games ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of data protection law, Lumiknot Games is the controller (or "data fiduciary") of the limited personal data described below. Google and Sentry act as independent controllers or as our processors for the services described in section 3.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Game progress: levels completed, stars, hints used, streak, and Daily puzzle results.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, theme.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (for example how many pulses a level took), used inside the app to balance difficulty.
  • Whether you own "Remove Ads" (a copy is also kept by Google Play so you can restore it).

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes this information permanently. Because there is no account, we cannot recover lost progress.

4. Why we process data (legal bases)

Where the EU/UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes, where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 5).
  • Legitimate interests — for crash reporting, keeping the app secure, preventing ad fraud, and showing non-personalised ads. You may object to this processing (section 9).
  • Performance of a contract — to provide and restore the "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records required by law, such as tax records held by Google Play.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process personal data on the basis of your consent or for legitimate uses permitted by that Act.

5. Ads and your choices

  • Sync shows two kinds of ads: ads between levels (full-screen ads that may appear after you finish a level, never in the middle of one and never in the Daily puzzle), and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to unlock a hint or restore a streak.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads between levels, and rewards are granted without any video.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, using Google's certified consent tool.
  • You can review or change your choices at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID on Android under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices). After deletion, apps cannot use it for personalised ads.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data for money, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed in section 3. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public. If Lumiknot Games or Sync is transferred to another owner, this policy will continue to apply to data collected under it.

Under some US state laws, allowing an ad network to use device identifiers for personalised advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. See section 10 for how to opt out.

7. International transfers

Google and Sentry may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. These providers use safeguards recognised by law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

8. Retention

  • On-device data: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): automatically deleted after at most 90 days.
  • Advertising data (Google): kept according to Google's retention policy.
  • Purchase records (Google Play): kept by Google as required for billing and legal purposes.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you, then deleted within 12 months.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • data portability;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights (India);
  • complain to your local data protection authority, or to the Data Protection Board of India.

Because Sync has no accounts, we usually hold no data that identifies you. Most data can be controlled directly: uninstall the app to remove on-device data, use Settings → Privacy choices for ads, and reset your Advertising ID. For anything else, email us. We respond within 30 days and may ask for information needed to locate your data.

10. US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We collect the following categories through the services in section 3: identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address), internet or device activity (ad interactions), approximate geolocation (from IP), and commercial information (purchase of "Remove Ads"). We do not collect sensitive personal information.

To opt out, use Settings → Privacy choices in the app, or delete your Advertising ID. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11. Children

Sync is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because Sync has no accounts, we do not hold information that identifies a child. If a child under 13 has used the app, a parent or guardian can remove its data by uninstalling the app (this deletes all on-device data) and deleting the device's Advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads). You can also contact us: we will delete any crash reports we can match to the device and time you describe, and help you use Google's tools to manage ad data.

12. Security

Data sent to the services listed above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit data collection to what the app needs.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will also note it in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
